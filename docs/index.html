Questions

Can I bring my Feedly or Inoreader feeds?

Yes. Import an OPML file and your folders come with it, nested categories included.

I use Mailbrew or CortadoMail. How do I switch?

Rebuild your sections in Compose, then point your newsletter subscriptions at your Brew address.

Is there a phone app?

Brew installs from the browser as an app. On a server or Cloudflare, Reeder, NetNewsWire and Unread can sync with it too.

What does “needs an add-on” mean?

Some places can’t do everything alone; a browser can’t receive email, for example. Brew tells you what to connect, or which mode does it natively.
