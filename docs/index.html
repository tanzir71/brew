Questions, answered

Local-first means honest tradeoffs.

Why not just use Feedly or Inoreader?+

They are polished cloud services, but require accounts and reserve many capabilities for paid plans. Brew is for one person who wants their feeds, rules, and reading history on their own machine.

What exactly is “a Brew”?+

A Brew is a daily digest assembled from sections you configure—such as five articles from Design, starred long reads, or stories selected by a rule. Generation freezes it, so the edition never changes under you.

Can I read it on my phone?+

Yes, while the computer running Brew is available on your local network. Brew is intentionally not a hosted sync service.

Does my computer need to stay on?+

It must be running for scheduled feed fetches and Brew generation. When it is off, nothing is fetched; it catches up on the next refresh.

How do backups work?+

Stop Brew and copy data/brew.db. That single SQLite file contains feeds, articles, rules, settings, and frozen editions. OPML export is also available for subscriptions.
